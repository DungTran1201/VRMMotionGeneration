# `exp/experiments/` — Experiment Logbooks & Notebooks

## Purpose
Maintains experiment logbooks, hypothesis descriptions, exploratory Jupyter notebooks, and trial configuration files.

## What Belongs Here
- Experiment tracking manifests detailing hyperparameter configs and test goals.
- Exploratory data analysis and model prototyping notebooks.

## What Must NOT Belong Here
- Checkpoint binary weights or large training datasets.
- Production application modules.

## Source-of-Truth Relationship
- Experiment findings are consolidated into formal reports in `exp/reports/`.
