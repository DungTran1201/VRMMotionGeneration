# `exp/reports/` — Experiment Evaluation Reports

## Purpose
Maintains formal markdown reports detailing experiment methodology, observed metrics, benchmark results, comparative evaluations, and promotion recommendations.

## What Belongs Here
- Benchmark reports (e.g., latency, kinematic fidelity, motion jitter).
- Model comparison reports (e.g., baseline vs fine-tuned).
- Experiment promotion proposals submitted for architectural review.

## What Must NOT Belong Here
- Raw binary dumps or unformatted log outputs.
- Unsubstantiated claims without recorded command outputs.

## Source-of-Truth Relationship
- Feeds into architecture decisions in `docs/05-architecture/` when proposing model promotions into the product.
