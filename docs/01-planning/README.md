# `docs/01-planning/` — Planning & Roadmaps

## Purpose
Maintains strategic project plans, development milestones, sprint scopes, delivery phase breakdowns, and work item backlogs.

## What Belongs Here
- Engineering roadmaps and phase execution plans.
- Milestone definitions and cross-functional deliverables.
- Migration and transition plans.

## What Must NOT Belong Here
- Raw requirements or technical domain models.
- Ephemeral personal notes or ad-hoc chat logs.

## Source-of-Truth Relationship
- Derives goals from `00-discovery/` and drives requirements elaboration in `02-requirements/`.
