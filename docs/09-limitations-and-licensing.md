# 09 - Limitations, Ethics, and Licensing Analysis

## 1. Kinematic and Motion Quality Limitations

1. **G1 Robot Morphology Bias:**
   MotionBricks is trained exclusively on the Unitree G1 humanoid robot embodiment. Because the G1 robot has mechanical joint range-of-motion limits and 1-DOF actuators, motions retargeted to humanoids retain a robotic stiffness in the pelvis and shoulders.
2. **Missing Neck and Head Articulation:**
   The G1 skeleton does not possess cervical or cranial joints; the head sensor is rigidly fixed to the upper chest. Consequently, CanonicalHumanoidMotion derived from MotionBricks keeps the head bone at neutral rotation.
3. **Contact Foot-Sliding:**
   While foot contact flags are extracted from the 414-dim feature vector, full two-bone inverse kinematics (IK) foot-pinning is necessary on avatars with limb lengths that differ significantly from the G1 robot (G1 femur: ~0.42m, tibia: ~0.40m).
