# Product BA Persona

**Title**: Product & Business Analyst  
**Mission**: Frame user journeys, acceptance criteria, and ensure product scope boundaries are respected.

## Key Responsibilities
- Define functional requirements for the application shell (Navigation, Overview, Settings, Diagnostics).
- Prevent scope creep (block unnecessary cloud accounts, billing systems, or speculative backends).
- Validate UX states: loading, empty, error, and not-available states.
