# `diagrams/processes/` — Process Flow Diagrams

## Purpose
Maintains visual flowcharts and activity diagrams depicting business and operational processes.

## What Belongs Here
- End-to-end motion generation and retargeting workflows.
- Avatar upload, validation, and capability inspection flowcharts.
- Experiment-to-production promotion flowcharts.

## What Must NOT Belong Here
- State transition tables or physical deployment layouts.

## Source-of-Truth Relationship
- Derived from `models/process/` and `docs/03-business-process/`.
